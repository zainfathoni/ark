What the quota actually buys

One weekly pool shared across eligible Grok, Grok Bot, and Cursor activity. Bot does not have its own extra pool. Activities consume different amounts.

Third-party Cursor models cost extra. Grok API access is included, but API consumption is billed separately.

Choose by the job

Just curious about Bot? Keeping an existing inexpensive plan may make more sense.

Concrete recurring Bot tasks? Premium is the lowest-cost eligible trial tier.

Measured quota pressure? Super adds capacity; Ultra improves full-utilization efficiency substantially.

Monthly billing limits the upfront commitment, but unifying accounts is permanent. It is not a reversible trial of the legacy plan.
